# MYRS Marketing Team

Comprehensive marketing automation suite with multi-agent orchestration for brand strategy, market research, persona development, content strategy, and digital asset creation.

## Overview

MYRS Marketing Team provides a complete end-to-end marketing strategy development system. Through progressive disclosure and quality gates, it guides you from initial discovery through final strategy synthesis, delivering professional marketing assets at each phase.

## Installation

Add the MYRS.ai marketplace first:

```bash
/plugin marketplace add jakreymyers/myrs-ai-marketplace
```

Then install MYRS Marketing Team:

```bash
/plugin install myrs-marketing-team@myrs-ai-marketplace
```

## What's Included

### 6 Specialized Agents

1. **Marketing Orchestrator** - Master coordinator for workflow governance
2. **Research Synthesizer** - Market research and competitive intelligence
3. **Persona Architect** - Customer persona and journey mapping
4. **Brand Strategist** - Brand positioning and voice development
5. **Content Strategist** - Content planning and messaging
6. **Website Architect** - Website design and conversion strategy

### 7-Phase Workflow

The plugin follows a comprehensive 7-phase process:

#### Phase 1: Discovery (1-2 days)
- Gather business context and define objectives
- **Deliverables**: Discovery brief

#### Phase 2: Research (3-5 days)
- Market research and competitive analysis
- **Deliverables**: Research brief, competitive matrix, market assessment

#### Phase 3: Personas (2-4 days)
- Develop detailed customer personas
- **Deliverables**: Persona cards, customer journey maps

#### Phase 4: Brand (2-3 days)
- Define brand strategy and voice
- **Deliverables**: Brand voice guide, narrative structure

#### Phase 5: Content (2-4 days)
- Create content strategy and messaging
- **Deliverables**: Content strategy, messaging matrix, content calendar

#### Phase 6: Website (3-5 days)
- Design website architecture and conversion strategy
- **Deliverables**: Website blueprint, page specifications, conversion optimization

#### Phase 7: Strategy Synthesis (1-2 days)
- Synthesize all phases into comprehensive strategy
- **Deliverables**: Complete marketing strategy document

### Commands

- `/myrs-marketing-team:start` - Begin marketing project workflow

## Key Features

- **Progressive Disclosure**: Gather information through targeted questions
- **Quality Gates**: Validation checkpoints at each phase transition
- **Multi-Agent Orchestration**: Specialized agents for each domain
- **Comprehensive Workflow**: Systematic 7-phase process

## Usage

After installation, invoke the Marketing Orchestrator to begin:

```bash
/myrs-marketing-team:start
```

The orchestrator will guide you through the complete workflow, delegating to specialist agents as needed.
