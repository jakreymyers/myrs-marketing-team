# Start Marketing Project

Initiate a new marketing project by activating the Marketing Orchestrator and beginning the discovery phase.

## What Happens

1. **Load Marketing Orchestrator**: Activate the chief coordinator agent
2. **Display Available Commands**: Show all orchestration commands
3. **Begin Discovery**: Start gathering business context
4. **Progressive Disclosure**: Guide through targeted questions
5. **Create Discovery Brief**: Document initial findings

## Process Flow

The Marketing Orchestrator will guide you through:

1. **Business Context**: Company, product, market
2. **Objectives**: Goals, KPIs, success criteria
3. **Target Audience**: Initial audience identification
4. **Scope & Priorities**: What to focus on first
5. **Resources & Timeline**: Available resources and deadlines

## Deliverables

- Discovery brief document
- Project context for subsequent phases
- Phase transition validation

## Next Steps

After completing discovery, the orchestrator will help you:
- Transition to Research phase
- Delegate to Research Synthesizer
- Begin market and competitive analysis

## Usage

```
/myrs-marketing-team:start
```

Then follow the interactive prompts to provide your business context.
